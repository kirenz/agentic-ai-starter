# Setting up the Nimbus Home Hub

Plug the hub into a power outlet near your router. The status light blinks blue while the hub starts, which takes about one minute.

Install the Nimbus app, sign in and tap "Add hub". Scan the QR code on the bottom of the hub to pair it with your account.

The hub supports up to 60 connected devices. Devices are added in the app under "Devices", then "Add device".
