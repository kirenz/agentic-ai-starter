# Wi-Fi and connectivity

The hub connects to 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks. If the status light is solid orange, the hub has lost its Wi-Fi connection.

To reset the Wi-Fi settings, hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the light blinks orange. Then add the hub again in the app. Devices and automations are kept.

If the connection drops repeatedly, move the hub closer to the router or switch the network band in the app under "Hub settings", then "Network".
